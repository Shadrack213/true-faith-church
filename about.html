Statement of Faith

Statement of Faith

How we see ourselves as believers of Christ Jesus

The Bible

We believe that the Holy Scriptures of the Old and New Testaments are the inspired Word of God, the supreme rule of faith, and contain all things necessary for salvation.

Scriptures: 2 Timothy 3:16–17; 2 Peter 1:20–21; Psalm 119:105.

God and Christ

We believe in one God, the Father Almighty, and in Jesus Christ as the mediator between God and mankind, through whom salvation and eternal life are made available to all who believe.

Scriptures: 1 Timothy 2:5; Deuteronomy 6:4; John 14:6; Acts 4:12.

The Holy Spirit

We believe that the Holy Spirit works through numerous spiritual gifts and talents within the church and empowers believers for Christian life and ministry.

Scriptures: John 14:26; Acts 1:8; 1 Corinthians 12:4–11; Galatians 5:22–23.

The Sabbath

We believe in the observance of the Sabbath as commanded by God and revealed through the Holy Scriptures. The church worships on Saturday as the holy day of rest and worship unto God.

Scriptures: Exodus 20:8–11; Genesis 2:2–3; Isaiah 58:13–14; Mark 2:27–28.

Baptism

We believe in baptism by immersion as the true expression of faith, repentance, and confession for every born-again believer.

Scriptures: Matthew 3:13–17; Mark 16:16; Acts 2:38; Romans 6:3–4.

Holy Communion

We believe that Holy Communion is a sacred means of grace through which believers partake in remembrance of the body and blood of Jesus Christ for the strengthening of faith.

Scriptures: Matthew 26:26–28; Luke 22:19–20; 1 Corinthians 11:23–26.

The Church

We believe that the church is the body of Christ, guided by the Holy Scriptures, united in one faith and one purpose, and governed according to biblical principles.

Scriptures: Ephesians 1:22–23; 1 Corinthians 12:12–13; Colossians 1:18.

Christian Life

We believe that Christians are called to live holy, sober, righteous, and godly lives in this present world while looking forward to the glorious appearing of Jesus Christ.

Scriptures: 1 Peter 1:15–16; Romans 12:1–2; Titus 2:11–13; Galatians 5:16.

Marriage

We believe in holy Christian marriage founded on biblical principles. The church upholds monogamous marriage, family unity, faithfulness, and moral purity.

Scriptures: Genesis 2:24; Matthew 19:4–6; Ephesians 5:22–33; Hebrews 13:4.

Heaven, Resurrection, and Eternal Life

We believe in the resurrection of both the just and the unjust at the second coming of Jesus Christ and in eternal life for the faithful believers of God.

Scriptures: John 5:28–29; 1 Thessalonians 4:16–17; Revelation 21:1–4; Daniel 12:2.

Evangelism

We believe in preaching, practicing, and propagating the Gospel of Jesus Christ through prayer, teaching, missions, and evangelism throughout the world.

Scriptures: Matthew 28:19–20; Mark 16:15; Acts 1:8; Romans 10:14–15.

Our History

True Faith Church International

True Faith Church International was founded through the divine calling and ministry of Apostle Samuel Brako of blessed memory, a native of Osiem in the Eastern Region of Ghana. The church traces its origin to the early 1900s when Christianity in the Gold Coast was largely dominated by churches that worshipped on Sunday. Through divine revelation, God raised Apostle Samuel Brako to restore the observance of the Sabbath and uphold biblical truth.

Apostle Samuel Brako, popularly known as Kwame Panin, was born into a royal family as the son of Nana Juaben Boateng I and Madam Abena Afrah. After the death of his father in 1904, he moved from Juaben to Osiem with his uncles, where he became an active and devoted member of the Methodist Church. He was known for his humility, discipline, hard work, and dedication to the things of God.

In the year 1914, while preparing to ring the Methodist church bell at dawn for morning service, Apostle Samuel Brako experienced a divine encounter. According to the church’s historical records, he was surrounded by a bright heavenly light and received a vision from angels instructing him to establish a Sabbath-observing church in Ghana. During the encounter, he was given a silver key as a symbol of divine authority and commission.

Following this revelation, he faced strong opposition, rejection, and persecution from both the church and members of his own family. Despite these challenges, he remained steadfast through fasting, prayer, and obedience to the voice of God.

In the same year, Pastor Sampson Krofa and Pastor David Krofa also received divine revelations concerning Sabbath worship. God directed them to seek out Apostle Samuel Brako at Osiem so they could work together to establish the new ministry. Their meeting marked the beginning of a united evangelistic movement devoted to worshipping God in spirit and in truth and observing the Sabbath according to Scripture.

The ministry continued to grow through prayer, fasting, evangelism, and baptism by immersion in rivers and streams, following the biblical example of the early church. Branches were established in many parts of Ghana including Amenase, Assin, Enyiresi, Obo Kwahu, Techimantia, Agogo, and Bododoma.

In 1924, the church became known as “The Faith Church.” Later, after reunification efforts among different groups within the ministry, a synod meeting held at Obo Kwahu in 1957 officially renamed the church “True Faith Church.” The headquarters was established at Kwahu Praso, and the church continued to expand both nationally and internationally.

Today, True Faith Church International continues to uphold the teachings of the Holy Scriptures, emphasizing holiness, Sabbath observance, prayer, evangelism, baptism by immersion, Christian discipline, and faithful service to God and humanity. The church has branches across Ghana and internationally, including the United States of America, Canada, Great Britain, Holland, Togo, and Côte d’Ivoire.
