About Us

About True Faith Church International

True Faith Church International was founded through the divine calling and ministry of Apostle Samuel Brako of blessed memory, a native of Osiem in the Eastern Region of Ghana. In 1914, through divine revelation, God raised him to restore the observance of the Sabbath and uphold biblical truth. Today the church has branches across Ghana and internationally.

Our Vision

To uphold the truth of God's Word through holiness and faithful Christian living.

Our Mission

Proclaiming the Gospel of Jesus Christ to all nations through Sabbath observance, prayer, and evangelism.

Sat 9–2

Sabbath Worship Service — Saturdays, 9:00 AM to 2:00 PM, in all branches.

Our Sabbath Worship Service is held on Saturdays from 9:00 AM to 2:00 PM in all branches.

We believe in the observance of the Sabbath as commanded by God and revealed through the Holy Scriptures. The church worships on Saturday as the holy day of rest and worship unto God (Exodus 20:8–11; Genesis 2:2–3; Isaiah 58:13–14; Mark 2:27–28).

Weekly fasting and prayer usually runs from 9:00 AM to 2:30 PM. The day varies by location, so please check with your local branch.

We believe in baptism by immersion as the true expression of faith, repentance, and confession for every born-again believer (Matthew 3:13–17; Mark 16:16; Acts 2:38; Romans 6:3–4).
